Fair by design — written into the generator

A runner that kills you with an impossible row is not hard, it is broken. These four rules are constraints in the level generator, not promises in a description.

  1. Every row leaves one clear lane, and it is never more than one lane away from where you already are. No double lane change is ever required.
  2. A "jump or slide" row never follows another one, so there is always reaction time between the two reflexes.
  3. Powerups only drop on the guaranteed-clear lane. A pickup is never bait.
  4. The opening stretch of every run is spawn-free while you settle into the speed.

The same track for everyone, every day

The Daily Challenge is generated from the calendar date, so every player in the world runs the identical obstacle sequence that day. Three daily missions rotate alongside it and pay out the moment you cross the line — there is no "tap to claim". Come back tomorrow and the streak flame grows.

Controls

Swipe left or right to change lane, up to jump, down to slide. That is the entire control scheme — there is nothing else to learn.

Honest about the limits

What Neorun 3D does not do:
  • No cloud saves. Your best score lives on the device and travels in an encrypted iPhone backup — it does not sync between two phones in use at once.
  • No multiplayer. The Daily Challenge is a shared track, not a live race.
  • Prisms picked up while running are the only currency. Nothing in the shop can be bought with money except removing ads.
  • Removing ads is one purchase, once. There is no subscription and there never will be.
