Privacy Policy

Neorun 3D: Overdrive

Last updated 17 September 2026 · Data controller: Oshi Lab, Switzerland

The short version

Neorun 3D has no account, no login and no server of mine. I do not receive any personal data from this app. Everything the game knows about you — your best score, your prisms, your unlocked craft, your streak — is written to storage on your own device and never sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Your best score, current prism balance and day streak
  • Which craft, worlds and trails you have unlocked, and which are selected
  • Your sound, haptics and graphics settings
  • Whether the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase has been made

This data is included in an encrypted iPhone or iPad backup if you make one. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

The one-time purchase that removes ads is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only asks Apple's StoreKit whether the purchase exists on this device, and StoreKit answers yes or no.

Advertising

Until the one-time ad-removal purchase is made, the free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, an independent advertising network. Google's handling of those ad requests is governed by Google's own privacy terms, not by this policy.

Two things are worth stating plainly:

  • You are asked before any tracking happens. On first launch the app presents Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline — or simply never allow it — the advertising identifier is not available to AdMob and the ads you see are not personalised. Declining costs you nothing in the game.
  • In the EEA and the UK, a consent form is shown first. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to collect the consent that GDPR requires before any personalised ad is requested. You can reopen it at any time from the app's settings.

The app also includes Apple's SKAdNetwork identifiers, which is how the App Store measures ad campaigns without revealing who you are: attribution is performed by Apple, and neither I nor the advertiser receives a user-level identifier from it.

You can limit personalised advertising further in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and › Apple Advertising. Buying the ad removal stops all ad requests permanently and the advertising code is never reached again.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, leaderboard scores and achievements are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not mine. Game Center is optional; the game is complete without it, and I receive nothing from it.

Children

Neorun 3D is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Analytics

There is no analytics product in Neorun 3D: no crash reporter, no attribution SDK, no telemetry, no product-analytics library. I do not measure how you play. The only third-party code in the app is Google's advertising and consent libraries described above, and they stop being reached once ads are removed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. They apply to personal data a controller holds about you — and I hold none from this app, so there is nothing for me to disclose or delete. You can erase everything the app stores at any time by deleting the app. If you want that confirmed in writing, ask me at contact@oshi-messenger.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version is published here before the change ships in an app update.
